# The Architecture of My Growth: An Educational Journey

## Roots and Foundations: Matriculation (2017–2019)

My formal scientific journey began at the secondary school level under the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Lahore (as shown in file 1000722613.jpg). Living in the small, rural village of Rajji Wala in Kasur presented an immediate logistical barrier: quality educational institutions were located miles away from home. Every single day required an exhausting, long-distance commute that tested my resolve before I even stepped into a classroom.

Despite these daily geographic and physical challenges, I channeled my energy entirely into my studies within the Science group. I achieved a total of **964 out of 1100 marks**, securing a **Grade-A+** (as seen in file 1000722613.jpg). ### The Symphony of Mathematics

While my grades were high across the board, Mathematics was where I found my true calling. Scoring 149 out of 150 was not merely a matter of rote memorization; it was a reflection of my deep fascination with logic. In a world full of variables, uncertainties, and long, grueling commutes from Rajji Wala, mathematics offered absolute certainty. It was an elegant language where every problem had a structured, logical solution waiting to be uncovered. This obsession with mathematical structures laid the groundwork for my eventual pivot into computing.

## The Language of Higher Science: Intermediate FSc (2019–2021)

Transitioning to higher secondary education, I joined CIMS College for Girls in Kasur to pursue FSc Pre-Engineering (as documented in file 1000722614.jpg). The college was located even further from my village, requiring grueling early morning journeys. Beyond the long travel distances, I faced a massive pedagogical shift: **the medium of instruction abruptly changed completely to English** for all science elective courses.

Adapting to rigorous engineering concepts while simultaneously mastering technical academic English was a silent battle. However, I adapted quickly, training my mind to think, analyze, and write strictly in English. My persistence culminated in a total score of **958 out of 1100** with an **A+ Grade** (as shown in file 1000722614.jpg):

## Engineering Abstract Ecosystems: BS Software Engineering (2022–2026)

To pursue my dream of engineering impactful technological ecosystems, I took a massive leap and crossed regional bounds to enroll at **Lahore College for Women University (LCWU)** for a **Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering** (as shown in file 1000722615.jpg).

Leaving Kasur for an entirely different city meant navigating intense logistical hurdles, exhausting inter-city travel, and the demands of an elite, fast-paced urban university environment. Conducted entirely in English, the rigorous four-year program pushed me to transform from a student of raw logic into a practical software builder. Overcoming a temporary setback in mathematical probability in my fourth semester, I pushed through to maintain a highly competitive academic profile, achieving a solid **Cumulative GPA of 3.16** (as documented in file 1000722615.jpg).
